## Description

Adversaries may leverage the network bandwidth resources of co-opted systems to complete resource-intensive tasks, which may impact system and/or hosted service availability. <br><br>Adversaries may also use malware that leverages a system's network bandwidth as part of a botnet in order to facilitate [[kb/mitre/attack/techniques/T1498-network-denial-of-service|Network Denial of Service]] campaigns and/or to seed malicious torrents.[^3]  Alternatively, they may engage in proxyjacking by selling use of the victims' network bandwidth and IP address to proxyware services.[^1]  Finally, they may engage in internet-wide scanning in order to identify additional targets for compromise.[^2] <br><br>In addition to incurring potential financial costs or availability disruptions, this technique may cause reputational damage if a victim’s bandwidth is used for illegal activities.[^1]
