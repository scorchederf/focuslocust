## Description

Adversaries may create email accounts that can be used during targeting. Adversaries can use accounts created with email providers to further their operations, such as leveraging them to conduct [[kb/mitre/attack/techniques/T1598-phishing-for-information|Phishing for Information]] or [[kb/mitre/attack/techniques/T1566-phishing|Phishing]].[^3]  Establishing email accounts may also allow adversaries to abuse free services – such as trial periods – to [[kb/mitre/attack/techniques/T1583-acquire-infrastructure|Acquire Infrastructure]] for follow-on purposes.[^2] <br><br>Adversaries may also take steps to cultivate a persona around the email account, such as through use of [[kb/mitre/attack/techniques/T1585.001-social-media-accounts|Social Media Accounts]], to increase the chance of success of follow-on behaviors. Created email accounts can also be used in the acquisition of infrastructure (ex: [[kb/mitre/attack/techniques/T1583.001-domains|Domains]]).[^3] <br><br>To decrease the chance of physically tying back operations to themselves, adversaries may make use of disposable email services.[^1]
